SHOPASSIST CUSTOMER SUPPORT POLICIES

RETURN POLICY

Customers can return eligible products within 30 days of delivery.

Products must be unused and returned in their original packaging.

Some products may not be eligible for return due to hygiene or safety restrictions.

Customers should initiate a return request through customer support.

REFUND POLICY

Refunds are processed after the returned product has been received and inspected.

Once approved, refunds are generally processed within 5 to 7 business days.

Refunds are issued to the original payment method.

SHIPPING POLICY

Standard delivery generally takes 3 to 7 business days.

Express delivery generally takes 1 to 2 business days.

Customers can track their order using their order ID.

CANCELLATION POLICY

Orders can generally be cancelled before they are shipped.

Once an order has been shipped, cancellation may no longer be possible.

Customers should contact customer support as soon as possible to request cancellation.

WARRANTY POLICY

Eligible electronic products are covered by a 12-month limited warranty.

The warranty covers manufacturing defects.

Physical damage caused by the customer is not covered under the standard warranty.

PAYMENT MODE

Payments can be made Online and Offline.

Online Payments include UPI, Net Banking and Debit and Credit Cards

Pay on Delivery and Cash on Delivery modes are also supported.
